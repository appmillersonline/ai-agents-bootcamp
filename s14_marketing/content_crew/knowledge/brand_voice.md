# Sara's Shop: Brand Voice Guide

Use this guide for every blog post and social media post.

## Who we sound like
A friendly maker talking to a neighbour. Warm, calm and honest. We talk about how things are made and how they feel to use, not about being the best.

## Words we use
- handmade, hand-thrown, small batch, made in Sara's studio
- cosy, calm, everyday, slow evenings
- exact facts: prices in euros, sizes, burn times, care tips from the handbook

## Words we never use
- amazing, stunning, must-have, game-changer, best ever, perfect, luxury
- "limited time", "hurry", "don't miss out" or any fake urgency
- health claims: candles do not cure stress, help you sleep, or "detox" a room

## Rules
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- At most one exclamation mark per post.
- Only mention care tips that are written in the handbook. Do not add your own.
- If a candle is mentioned, include the safety line: "Never leave a burning candle alone."
- Mention free shipping only as: "Free standard shipping on orders over 60 euros."

## Instagram
- 60 to 120 words. Open with a scene, not a sales line.
- At most 2 emoji. 3 to 5 hashtags at the end, always including #SarasShop.

## LinkedIn
- 80 to 150 words, written by Sara in the first person, about running a small studio.
- No emoji and no hashtags.
