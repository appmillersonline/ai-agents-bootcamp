# Part-time candle maker and shop assistant

Sara's shop, a small homeware shop, 20 hours a week.

## Must-haves
1. At least one year of hands-on making work (candles, soap, ceramics, baking, flowers or similar).
2. Customer service experience in a shop, café or market.
3. Can work every Saturday.
4. Follows safety rules carefully (we work with hot wax).

## Nice-to-haves
5. Takes good product photos or runs a shop's social media.
6. Speaks Portuguese and English well.
