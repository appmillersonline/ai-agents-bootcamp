# Sara's Shop: Customer Service Handbook

Sara's Shop is a small online shop selling handmade homeware: mugs, vases, candles, tote bags and tea towels. Most pieces are made by Sara in her studio; candles and textiles come from three small partner makers. This handbook is the single source of truth for anyone answering customers, human or AI.

## 1. Our voice

- Warm, short and clear. Write like a friendly shop owner, not a call centre.
- Use the customer's first name once, near the start.
- Never blame the customer. Never argue about a review.
- Say sorry once, sincerely, when something went wrong. Do not repeat it.
- No exclamation marks in apologies. At most one exclamation mark per message.
- Sign off as "Sara's Shop" unless Sara herself is writing.

## 2. Products and materials

### Mugs
- Blue mug set (two mugs), 24 euros. Stoneware, 350 ml each. Dishwasher safe on the top rack. Microwave safe. Not oven safe.
- Speckled mug, 14 euros. Stoneware, 300 ml. Dishwasher safe on the top rack.
- Every mug is hand-thrown, so sizes vary by up to 5 percent and the glaze is never identical. This is part of the charm, not a fault.

### Vases
- Ceramic vase, 45 euros. Hand-thrown, 22 cm tall, glazed inside so it holds water.
- Bud vase, 18 euros. 10 cm tall. Holds water.
- Vases are not dishwasher safe. Wash by hand with warm soapy water.

### Candles
- Candle trio, 22 euros. Three soy wax candles: lavender, vanilla and cedar. About 25 hours of burning each.
- Lavender candle, 12 euros. Soy wax, about 40 hours.
- Trim the wick to 5 mm before each use. Never leave a burning candle alone. Burn for no more than 3 hours at a time.

### Textiles
- Linen tote bag, 18 euros. Natural linen, 38 by 42 cm. Machine wash cold, hang to dry.
- Tea towel, 9 euros. Linen and cotton. Machine wash at 40 degrees.

## 3. Shipping

- We ship within the EU and to the UK, Norway and Switzerland.
- Orders placed by 2 pm on a weekday are packed the same day. Orders after 2 pm, or at weekends, are packed the next weekday.
- Standard shipping: 4.90 euros, 2 to 4 working days in our country, 4 to 7 working days elsewhere in the EU.
- Free standard shipping on orders over 60 euros.
- Express shipping: 9.90 euros, next working day in our country only, if ordered by 2 pm.
- UK, Norway and Switzerland: 12 euros, 5 to 9 working days. The customer may have to pay import fees; we say this clearly before checkout.
- Every parcel has tracking. The tracking link is in the shipping email.
- Ceramics are packed in recycled paper and corrugated card. We do not use plastic bubble wrap.

### Late parcels
- If a parcel is 3 working days later than the estimate, apologise once and check the tracking.
- If tracking has not moved for 5 working days, open a ticket with the carrier and tell the customer we have done so.
- If a parcel is lost (no movement for 10 working days), send a replacement or a full refund, the customer's choice. This needs Sara's approval if the order is over 60 euros.
- If shipping took more than 2 working days longer than promised, we refund the shipping cost without being asked. This does not need approval.

## 4. Returns

- Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.
- The customer pays return shipping, unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.
- Candles can only be returned unopened and unused, for safety and hygiene.
- Personalised or custom pieces cannot be returned unless faulty.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return arriving.
- We do not give cash refunds for items bought with a gift card. These get a new gift card.

## 5. Damaged or faulty items

- Ask for one photo of the damage and one of the box. Never ask for more than two photos.
- Cracked or broken ceramics: offer a replacement or a full refund, the customer's choice. The customer does not need to send the broken item back.
- Small glaze marks, size differences and colour variation are normal for handmade pieces. Explain this kindly; offer a 10 percent goodwill discount code if the customer is still unhappy.
- Refunds or replacements up to 20 euros can be done straight away. Anything over 20 euros needs Sara's approval first.
- Log every damaged item in the damage sheet with the order number, item, carrier and photo.

## 6. Discounts and codes

- WELCOME10: 10 percent off a first order. Only for new customers who joined the newsletter.
- SORRY10: 10 percent goodwill code. Only Sara or the support lead can give it.
- Codes cannot be combined. Codes do not apply to shipping.
- We never promise a discount, free item or refund in a public review reply.
- Price matching: we do not price match. Explain that every piece is handmade.

## 7. Orders and changes

- Customers can change an address or cancel an order until it is packed. After that, it is a return.
- To change an order, the customer must give the order number and the email used for the order. Never change an order without both.
- We never share any customer's details with another customer, ever. If someone asks about an order that is not theirs, say we can only discuss an order with the person who placed it.

## 8. Wholesale and custom orders

- Wholesale enquiries go to Sara by email. Do not quote wholesale prices.
- Custom pieces take 4 to 6 weeks. A 50 percent deposit is needed to start. Sara confirms every custom order personally.
- Corporate gifts over 20 pieces: pass to Sara.

## 9. When to hand over to Sara

Hand the conversation to Sara (and tell the customer she will reply within one working day) when:
- the customer is very upset, or mentions a lawyer, the press or social media;
- a refund or replacement is over 20 euros;
- the question is about wholesale, custom pieces or corporate gifts;
- you are not sure of the answer. Never guess. Saying "I'll check with Sara" is always better than a wrong answer.

## 10. Opening hours and contact

- Customer service answers Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.
- Messages at weekends are answered on Monday.
- Email: hello@saras-shop.example
- The studio is not open to visitors, except on the first Saturday of each month, 10 am to 2 pm.

## 11. Care cards

Every parcel includes a small care card. If a customer lost it, the care instructions are:
- Ceramics: hand wash vases, top rack for mugs, avoid sudden temperature changes such as pouring boiling water into a cold mug straight from the fridge.
- Candles: trim the wick, burn on a heat-proof surface, keep away from children, pets and drafts.
- Linen: wash cold, hang dry, iron while slightly damp.

## 12. Things we never do

- Never invent a policy that is not in this handbook.
- Never promise a delivery date the carrier has not given.
- Never share another customer's name, email, address or order.
- Never offer a refund, discount or free item over 20 euros without Sara's approval.
- Never reply to a review with a promise of money.
