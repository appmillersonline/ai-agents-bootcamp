# Sara's Shop: Staff Guide (sample)

## Working hours
The studio is staffed Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00. The market stall runs on Saturdays from 8:00 to 14:00. Packing for the day ends at 15:30 so parcels make the 16:00 courier collection.

## Holidays
Each part-time team member gets 20 days of paid holiday a year, pro rata. Ask Sara at least two weeks ahead. No holidays in the two weeks before Christmas, our busiest time.

## Sick days
If you're sick, message Sara before 8:30. You don't need a doctor's note for the first three days.

## Packing standards
Mugs and vases are wrapped in two layers of paper and a corrugated sleeve. Candles go in a box with paper fill, never loose. Every parcel gets a care card and a handwritten thank-you.

## Breakages in the studio
Write every breakage in the logbook with the date and item. Never sell a chipped piece as new; chipped pieces go to the seconds shelf at half price.
