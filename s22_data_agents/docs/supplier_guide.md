# Sara's Shop: Supplier Guide (sample)

## Our suppliers
Clayworks Studio makes the mug sets and vases. Wick & Co makes the candles. Northloom weaves the tote bags and tea towels.

## Ordering
Reorders go out on Mondays and Thursdays. Always order in full packs: mugs in packs of 6, vases in packs of 4, candles in packs of 12, totes in packs of 10.

## Payment terms
Clayworks and Northloom are paid within 30 days of the invoice. Wick & Co is paid within 14 days. Never pay an invoice from a new bank account without phoning the supplier first.

## Lead times
Clayworks takes about three weeks, because every piece is glazed and fired by hand. Wick & Co takes one week. Northloom takes two weeks.

## Quality problems
Photograph any faulty delivery on arrival and email the supplier within 7 days. Keep the faulty items until the supplier says what to do.
