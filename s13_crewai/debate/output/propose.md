1. **It saves time and lets small shops stay responsive.**  
   Many small shops don’t have a full-time customer service team, so emails pile up fast. An AI agent can answer common questions instantly—like opening hours, order status, return policies, and stock checks—so customers get replies in minutes instead of days.

2. **It helps small businesses compete with bigger retailers.**  
   Large companies already offer fast support. Small shops can close that gap with AI without hiring extra staff. For example, a local clothing store can use AI to handle routine questions overnight, giving customers the same quick service they expect from major brands.

3. **It cuts costs while freeing staff for higher-value work.**  
   Instead of spending hours on repetitive emails, shop owners and employees can focus on sales, product sourcing, and in-person service. That improves the business overall. A bakery owner, for instance, can spend less time replying to “Do you deliver?” emails and more time running the shop and serving customers.
