1. **Small shops lose the personal touch that makes them special.**  
   Customers often choose a small shop because they want a human relationship, not a scripted reply. A local florist or bakery can build loyalty by answering personally, but an AI message can feel cold and generic.

2. **AI can make mistakes that hurt trust and sales.**  
   If it gives the wrong opening hours, stock info, return policy, or delivery details, customers get frustrated fast. For a small shop, one bad reply can mean losing a repeat customer, and there may be no full support team to fix it quickly.

3. **It can create more work, not less.**  
   Small shops still need to monitor, correct, and train the AI. When the issue is unusual or sensitive—complaints, refunds, special orders—the owner must step in anyway. For a small business, spending time cleaning up AI errors can be worse than simply answering the emails directly.
