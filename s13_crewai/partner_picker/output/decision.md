# Sara’s First Wholesale Pitch Recommendation

## Chosen business: **The Coffee**

### One-sentence push notification
**Sara, pitch The Coffee first — it has the strongest active Lisbon expansion signal and the best near-term chance for a wholesale candle win.**

### Why I chose it
- **Strongest immediate growth signal:** The Coffee has at least 9 operating Lisbon locations, 2 coming soon, and additional Lisbon expansion planned, which makes it the clearest near-term wholesale opportunity.
- **Best operational fit for candles:** A multi-location café chain is a strong fit for a repeatable wholesale product like candles, especially if Sara wants a scalable account.
- **Higher likelihood of quick traction:** Because it is already operating and expanding in Lisbon, it is more likely to be ready for vendor outreach now than a business still in pre-opening mode.
- **Clear momentum across the business:** The hiring and supply-chain-related signals suggest the company is actively building capacity, which is a good sign for new supplier conversations.

## Businesses not chosen
### Delta Cafés / Delta Coffee House Experience
- Strong momentum and recent Lisbon activations.
- However, the available research does **not** show clear supplier-preference or local-sourcing signals.
- It looks promising, but slightly less compelling than The Coffee for a first wholesale pitch.

### The Standard, Lisbon
- Attractive premium hospitality target with strong guest-experience positioning.
- But the opening appears to be delayed to **Q1 2027**, so the timing is less immediate.
- Also, there is no confirmed supplier list or clear procurement signal in the provided context.

## Final recommendation
**Pitch The Coffee first.** It combines the strongest current expansion momentum with the most practical near-term chance of converting a wholesale candle account.
