# Sara's shop: customer FAQ

## Shipping
- Orders leave our studio within 2 working days.
- Delivery takes 2 to 4 working days at home, and 5 to 10 working days abroad.
- Shipping is free on orders over €40. Below that, it's €4.90.

## Returns
- You can return unused items in their original box within 30 days.
- You pay the return postage, unless the item arrived damaged or wrong.
- We refund within 5 working days after the item reaches us.

## Damaged or wrong items
- Send us a photo within 7 days, with your order number.
- We send a free replacement, or a full refund if you prefer.

## Changing or cancelling an order
- You can change or cancel an order while its status is "processing".
- Once it has shipped, please use a return instead.

## Products
- Candles are soy wax and burn for about 40 hours. Trim the wick before lighting.
- Mugs are dishwasher safe. Vases are handmade, so each one is a little different.

## Talking to a person
- Sara answers tickets Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, usually within one working day.
