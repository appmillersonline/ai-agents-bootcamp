# Sara's Shop: IT Guide (sample)

## Printer
The studio printer is the "Studio-Laser" on the shop wifi. If it won't print: turn it off and on, check it shows "Ready", then remove and re-add it in your laptop's printer settings. Paper jams: open the back flap, never pull paper from the front.

## Wifi
Staff use the "Shop-Staff" network. The password is on the card in the office drawer; never post it in chat or email. Customers use "Shop-Guest".

## VPN
Use the VPN only when working from home. If it keeps dropping, check the status page first, then restart the VPN app.

## Slow laptop
Restart it fully (not just close the lid). Close browser tabs you don't need. If it's still slow after a restart, open a ticket.

## Passwords
Never share your password with anyone, including IT and including this helpdesk. Forgotten passwords: use the self-service reset page. If that fails, IT can reset it after checking who you are; that needs a person to approve it.

## New starters
A new starter needs: an email account, a laptop and a printer setup. Sara requests these at least three days before the start date.
